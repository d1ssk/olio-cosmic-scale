# Sun texture

`sun.jpg`: unmodified 2048×1024 Solar System Scope Sun texture.
Downloaded 2026-09-10 from https://www.solarsystemscope.com/textures/download/2k_sun.jpg
Source: https://www.solarsystemscope.com/textures/

Rendered as illustrative surface imagery, not a map for the selected date. The shader adds low-amplitude procedural brightness evolution and illustrative limb darkening without moving the physical surface. No corona or bloom expands the physical silhouette.
